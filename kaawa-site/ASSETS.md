# Asset provenance and generation prompts

All six photographs were created with the built-in image generation tool for this project on 22 October 2026. They are demonstration assets, not photographs of actual KAAWA products or premises. Optimized JPEG copies are stored in `dist/assets/`. The cup is an original layered SVG illustration embedded in `app.js`, also a demonstration packaging design. Original generated PNGs remain in the generation output directory.

## Prompt set

- **latte.jpg** — Premium photographic campaign image for KAAWA. Portrait 1024×1536. Clear takeaway glass of iced Spanish latte, espresso swirling through creamy milk, large translucent ice cubes and condensation. Electric cobalt blue sleeve with exact white bold text “KAAWA.”. Matching cobalt background and tabletop, hard side sunlight, medium-format editorial photography, full centered glass, no lid, straw or other objects. Demonstration brand asset.
- **matcha.jpg** — Portrait premium studio photograph of clear glass iced date syrup matcha latte, deep green matcha marbling through creamy milk, amber syrup at base, ice and condensation. Small cobalt sleeve with exact white text “KAAWA.” Pale sage seamless backdrop and table, hard natural side light, elegant shadow, full centered glass, no props. Demonstration product asset.
- **v60.jpg** — Portrait premium studio photograph of iced black filter coffee in a clear tumbler on warm terracotta tabletop and backdrop. Dark amber coffee, large ice, sunlight caustics and condensation. Small blue label reading “KAAWA.” in white bold letters. Centered whole glass, minimal editorial campaign, no props.
- **cookie.jpg** — Landscape premium macro food photograph. Oversized thick chocolate chunk cookie broken into two rough organic halves, gooey dark chocolate exposed and slightly stretched, flaky salt and golden crust. Rich brown tabletop, strong side lighting and realistic texture, 40-degree overhead camera, no plates, hands or text.
- **cafe.jpg** — Wide premium architectural editorial photograph of an imagined specialty café. Warm daylight through large left window, pale travertine counter, espresso machine, cobalt blue stools, warm oak furniture, cream plaster, sculptural pendants and plants. No people, text or logos. Not an actual location.
- **night.jpg** — Edit the exact cafe.jpg composition to blue hour. Preserve every object, furniture placement and camera framing. Deep blue twilight outside, remove sunlight patches. Pendants emit localized amber pools, shelf accent lights glow, espresso shadows and cinematic highlights. No flat black overlay or new objects.

GSAP/ScrollTrigger downloaded from jsDelivr's npm GSAP 3.12.5 distribution. Their original license headers are preserved.
